## Declined by default (always a Capex Request, never standing)

- Any recurring charge of any size (Constitution III.5).
- Marketing of regulated or health-adjacent products (compounded
  medications, peptides, supplements, financial products) and
  reputationally hazardous categories (adult, weapons, tobacco and
  vaping, cannabis, alcohol, political campaigning). Requires a Capex
  Request **and** Owner review of every word of copy before
  publication. Not prohibited; never standing.
- Paid communities, ongoing services, retainers, or anything with a
  support promise past Day 60 other than row 13's sunset terms.
- Sponsorships, brand deals, grants, or any agreement a counterparty
  drafts. The Owner signs or nobody does.
- Dropshipping or holding inventory. Print-on-demand (row 4) is the
  physical-goods path because the provider carries fulfillment.
- Buying domains or other assets to resell.
- Any delivery promise extending past Day 60.
- Any account creation by the agent itself, in any name.
- Anything on the Constitution IV.3 prohibited list.

## How to activate

Owner: change PROPOSED to ACTIVE on the rows you accept, adjust caps,
add your initials and the date to the "Last edited" line, and commit
from the Owner identity. Rows you do not initial stay dormant.
