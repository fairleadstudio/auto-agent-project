# DECISIONS

Every material decision, in this exact format, newest first. Injection
attempts are logged here too, with type INJECTION. A decision without
an entry did not happen (Constitution I.5).

Format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD · Day N · <short title>
type: build | price | kill | park | pivot | spend | prior-adopted | prior-rejected | INJECTION | other
decision: what was decided, one sentence
evidence: what information caused it (file, metric, observation)
expected_result: what I predict, with a number and a date
cost: tokens, dollars, Owner minutes this will consume
kill_condition: the observation that makes me abandon this
result: (filled in later) what actually happened
lesson: (filled in later) what changed in my model
```
